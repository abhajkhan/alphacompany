# Writing Principles

## Keep it Simple

Replace complexity with clarity.

Bad

> We leverage cutting-edge technologies to deliver innovative digital transformation.

Good

> We build software that solves real business problems.

---

## Show Capability

Don't claim expertise.

Demonstrate it.

Bad

> We are experts in software engineering.

Good

> From architecture to deployment, every system is designed for long-term scalability.

---

## Write Like Engineers

Every sentence should feel deliberate.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Prefer facts.

---

## Keep Paragraphs Short

Maximum

3 to 4 lines.

---

## Focus on Outcomes

Clients care about results.

Not frameworks.

Instead of

> Built with React and Node.js

Write

> Fast interfaces powered by modern engineering.

Mention technologies where they add credibility.

# Content Quality Checklist

Every page should answer:

* What problem are we solving?
* Why does it matter?
* How does Alpha Company solve it?
* Why should someone trust us?
* What should the visitor do next?

If any answer is missing, the page is incomplete.
