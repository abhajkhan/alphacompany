# Alpha Company Website Architecture v1.0

## Website Philosophy

Alpha Company is positioned as an **Engineering Studio** that creates software products and partners with businesses to design, build, and scale digital platforms.

The website should communicate three ideas:

1. We build our own products.
2. We engineer custom software for ambitious companies.
3. We think deeply about technology and share that knowledge.

The navigation should feel minimal, while the content hierarchy naturally guides visitors from curiosity to confidence to conversion.

# Information Architecture Principles

1. **Products prove capability.** Building your own products demonstrates engineering maturity beyond client work.

2. **Services generate revenue.** They should be easy to discover and understand without dominating the narrative.

3. **Case studies provide evidence.** Every significant service should be backed by at least one real implementation.

4. **Insights establish authority.** Publishing thoughtful technical content compounds trust over time and improves organic search visibility.

5. **Every page should have a single primary objective.** Avoid pages that try to educate, sell, recruit, and entertain simultaneously.

6. **Every journey ends with a clear next step.** Whether it's scheduling a consultation, requesting a demo, reading a case study, or applying for a role, no page should leave visitors wondering what to do next.

This architecture positions Alpha Company as an engineering-led product studio rather than a generic software agency. It balances product innovation, service offerings, and technical thought leadership into a cohesive experience that can scale as the company grows.
